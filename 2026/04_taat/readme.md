# Event Title

### Taat: Making of - From idea to Steam!

## Topic Description

Hello Everyone!
We are excited to invite you to our next SKG Java Meetup, where one of our community members will share his journey of building and publishing a game on Steam! 🎮

### Topic
Side projects are easy to start. Finishing them is the real challenge.
And when it comes to games... making it to Steam is a whole different level!

Taat started as a simple joke project, inspired by a YouTube tutorial, and evolved into a fully released Sokoban-style puzzle game with 100 handcrafted levels, achievements, cloud saves, and Steam integration!

In this talk, we’ll take a journey through 5 years of building a game from scratch - without prior game development experience - and explore what it really takes to turn a Game idea into a finished product.

We will cover:
- The motivation behind creating a PC game as a side project
- Choosing the right programming language and engine (Lua / LOVE2D)
- Learning (way) too many new things along the way
- Game and level design challenges
- The famous “ninety–ninety rule” in practice
- A retrospective: what went right (and wrong)
- What’s next after publishing on Steam
💡 Whether you’re working on your own side project, thinking of starting one, or just curious about game development, this session will give you a real-world, honest perspective.

👉 You can check out the game here:
https://store.steampowered.com/app/3220160/taat/
