# Event Title

### The Elegant Monolith: Modern Application Architecture with Spring Modulith

## Topic Description

Hello Everyone!
We are excited to invite you to our next meetup, where we will explore how to bring order, scalability, and elegance back to monolithic architectures using Spring Modulith! 🚀

### Topic
Everyone in the industry talks about microservices, but they come with massive operational complexity and aren't always the right fit. On the flip side, traditional monoliths often devolve into a tangled "big ball of mud" over time. But did you know that monoliths don’t have to be messy?
P.S. Monoliths Are Not Dead yet!

In this talk, we will explore:
- The core concepts of the Modular Monolith architecture
- How Spring Modulith helps you structure your app into clean, business-driven modules
- How to enforce architectural boundaries and keep things under control with simple tests
- Event-driven communication between internal application modules
- Live examples showcasing how to keep your codebase clean and maintainable
💡 Whether you are struggling with a messy legacy app, considering a migration to microservices, or starting a greenfield project, this session will offer valuable insights into keeping your architecture elegant and simple.

### 🎤 Speaker
We have the honor to welcome George Charitakis (https://www.linkedin.com/in/gecharita/) Software & Cloud Architect @PRODYNA who will be presenting the topic! George will guide us through the concepts and practical applications of Spring Modulith to help us build better and more sustainable software.

🎯 Whether you are a Java developer, Software Architect, Tech Lead, or simply curious about modern application design, you will find this session highly useful, and it might just change how you view your architecture!
