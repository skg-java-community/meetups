# Event Title

### The Power of GitOps and the Rise of ArgoCD

## Topic Description

Hello Everyone!

We are excited to invite you to our first meetup of the year, featuring a very special guest who will demonstrate to us the magic of GitOps and ArgoCD!

## Topic
Everyone is talking about GitOps, CI/CD pipelines, automated deployments, and cloud-native workflows. Yet, in practice, many teams still struggle with the fundamentals or misunderstand how GitOps is meant to work.

In this talk, we will explore:

The core principles of GitOps
The end-to-end GitOps flow
Key benefits and real-world challenges
A quick introduction to ArgoCD, followed by a live demo showcasing the elegance of GitOps in action
Common anti-patterns observed while helping organizations adopt GitOps and ArgoCD
💡 Whether you are just getting started or already using GitOps in production, this session will offer valuable insights and practical takeaways.

🎤 Speaker
Our very own Patroklos Papapetrou (https://www.linkedin.com/in/ppapapetrou/) Lead Open Source Engineer at Octopus Deploy - will be presenting the topic!
Patroklos is the founder of our SKG Java Community, which he started 12 years ago - for which we are very grateful 🙏 - so this talk is extra special for us!

🎯 Whether you are a Java developer, DevOps engineer, Architect, or simply curious about GitOps, we would love to see you there!
